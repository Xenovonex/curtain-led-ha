**Sprites.** The patterns in the drawing library (`draw-studio/index.html`) are
original, hand-made pixel art. Some simple shapes may evoke well-known video-game
or pop-culture characters; they are given generic names and are not affiliated
with, endorsed by, or copied from any rights holder. If you believe a specific
sprite is too close to a protected work, open an issue and it will be removed or
adjusted.

**Flags** (US, Puerto Rico, Netherlands, Curaçao) are national flags in the
public domain.

**Dependencies.** The Python libraries `bleak`, `bleak-retry-connector`, and
`paho-mqtt` are installed via pip and are not included in this repository; they
retain their own licenses.

**Protocol.** The BLE protocol in `docs/PROTOCOL.md` was reverse-engineered
independently from Bluetooth HCI captures of the author's own device. This
project is unaffiliated with any light manufacturer or app.
